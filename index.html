BEFORE YOU GO

A few things
you might ask.

Have another question? Chat with us on WhatsApp.

Can I customise a tour?

Yes. Share your preferred destinations, dates, interests and budget so we can discuss a suitable itinerary.

How do I get a taxi quote?

Send your pickup and drop locations, travel date, one-way or return preference, group size and luggage needs.

Are prices shown on the website?

Quotes depend on travel dates, route, vehicle, accommodation and availability. Contact us for a quote with clear inclusions.

Are hotels and meals included?

Inclusions are specific to your agreed plan. Ask for the written list of hotels, meals, transfers, tickets and exclusions before confirming.

What about cancellations?

Ask for the cancellation and refund terms for your selected services before paying. Terms may depend on the hotel, vehicle provider and travel dates.

Does the enquiry form confirm a booking?

No. It prepares a message for WhatsApp. Your booking is confirmed separately after the plan, availability and terms are agreed.
